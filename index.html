A message from SORT Founder — Bill Graw

I achieved my dream of being an entrepreneur with a diverse portfolio: a restaurant chain, a fleet of food trucks, a bison farm, and even a US patent. I tasted success, but it came at a cost – my time. I was drowning in the chaos of running multiple businesses.

One evening, while frantically searching for my to-do list, I asked my wife for help. "Was it on a blue piece of paper, written in sharpie?" she inquired. "Yes!" I replied. Her response: "It went through the wash." It was at that moment I realized there had to be a better way.

Surprisingly, there's no solid app or system out there that really solves the problem of small businesses drowning in information. So, I decided to create it for myself.

The inspiration? Oddly, a graduate school lecture on the Second Law of Thermodynamics – the tendency towards increasing disorder, more commonly referred to as "Entropy". As crazy as it sounds, I saw the parallel between the universe's drift towards chaos and the daily chaos of running a small business. I set out to build a tool to conquer the daily entropy business owners face.

The result was SORT.

Now, SORT helps small business owners like me reclaim their lives, providing a simple, effective way to manage everything. My employees love it too, enjoying a clear, easy-to-use standardized system.

Let SORT work for you.

To learn more about SortSoftware and the SORT App, visit us at sortforms.com.

Sincerely, Bill Graw
