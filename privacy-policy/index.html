Privacy Policy

SORTSOFTWARE.COM — Last updated September 01, 2024

This Privacy Notice for SortSoftware describes how we access, collect, store, use, and share personal information when you use our services, including our mobile application Sort Forms and related business activities. Questions or concerns should be directed to help@sortsoftware.com. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our services.

Summary of Key Points

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide. We do not process sensitive information or collect data from third parties. Information is processed to provide our services, improve our offerings, communicate with you, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements. Personal information may be shared during business transfers. We maintain organizational and technical security measures, though no system is guaranteed 100% secure. You have privacy rights depending on your location, most easily exercised through a data subject access request.

What Information Do We Collect?

Information you voluntarily provide to us may include:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Mailing addresses
  • Debit/credit card numbers
  • Usernames
  • Passwords

Payment data is processed through Stripe. You must ensure all information you provide is accurate and notify us of any changes.

How Do We Process Your Information?

We process information for account creation and authentication, service delivery, user support and inquiry responses, administrative communications, and order fulfillment and management.

When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

Information may be shared during business transfers, mergers, or acquisitions.

How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We retain information only as long as necessary for the stated purposes, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Information is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed, or securely stored and isolated if deletion isn't possible.

How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We implement appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures, though no electronic transmission is absolutely secure. You access our services at your own risk, and should only do so in a secure environment.

Do We Collect Information from Minors?

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 or market to them. By using our services, you represent that you are at least 18, or are a parent/guardian consenting on behalf of a minor. If we learn we have collected data from a minor, we deactivate the account and delete the data.

What Are Your Privacy Rights?

You may review, change, or terminate your account depending on your location, and may withdraw consent by contacting us. You can request account termination; we will deactivate or delete your account from our active databases but may retain information to prevent fraud or comply with legal requirements.

Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

We do not currently respond to Do-Not-Track browser signals, as no uniform technology standard exists for recognizing such signals.

Do United States Residents Have Specific Privacy Rights?

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Montana, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia may have rights to access, receive details about, correct inaccuracies in, delete, or obtain copies of their personal information. We do not collect various categories of sensitive personal information, including biometric information or geolocation data. We have not disclosed, sold, or shared personal information to third parties for business purposes in the preceding 12 months, and commit not to sell information in the future.

Your rights include: knowing whether your data is being processed, accessing it, correcting inaccuracies, requesting deletion, obtaining copies of previously shared data, non-discrimination for exercising your rights, and opting out of targeted advertising, sales, or profiling. Depending on your state of residence, you may also obtain a list of third parties who received data disclosures or limit sensitive data use.

How to Exercise Your Rights

You can exercise your rights by submitting a data subject access request, emailing help@sortsoftware.com, or using the contact details provided below. Authorized agents may submit requests with valid proof of authorization.

Request Verification

We verify your identity before processing requests using information provided in the request, or by requesting additional information for security purposes.

Appeals

You may appeal a declined request by emailing help@sortsoftware.com. You will receive written notification of the actions taken or not taken, with an explanation.

California "Shine The Light" Law

California residents can request free, annual information about categories of personal data disclosed to third parties for direct marketing purposes.

Do We Make Updates to This Notice?

We update this notice as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws. Updates are indicated by a revised date, and we encourage you to review this notice frequently.
